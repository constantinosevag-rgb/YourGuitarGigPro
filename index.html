Info & Help

How to Use

  • Setlists: Create folders to organize your gig. Drag & Drop songs to reorder.
  • Loading: Drag files or folders directly, or use the buttons. Supports PDF & Images.
  • Performance: Hit Play or Spacebar to scroll. Slider controls speed (1-15).
  • Tools: Use the Menu (☰) for the Tuner and Chords.

Motivation

I created YourGuitarGig to empower guitarists and musicians with a distraction-free, all-in-one digital stage companion. No more messy papers, no more separate tuner apps, and no more panic during a live set. Just you and your music.

Requirements

  • Modern Web Browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge).
  • No installation required. Runs offline after loading.
