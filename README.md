# 🎸 YourGuitarGig PRO

**The ultimate distraction-free setlist/gig manager for live performers.**

YourGuitarGig PRO is a web-based offline application designed to replace messy paper setlists and complex expensive software. It allows musicians to load sheet music, lyrics, or chords (PDF & Images), organize them instantly, and perform with seamless auto-scrolling live.

---

## 💡 Motivation
This project was born out of a simple need: **Focus.**
As a musician, I was tired of shuffling through papers, dealing with heavy apps that require internet, subscriptions, or panicking when a setlist changed last minute. I wanted a tool that is:
* **Fast:** Loads instantly.
* **Simple:** No login, no ads, no bloated menus.
* **Reliable:** Works on any device with a browser.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📂 Setlist Management
* **Universal Support:** Loads **PDFs** and **Images** (.jpg, .png).
* **Drag & Drop Organization:** Easily rearrange songs or move them between folders. A visual blue line shows you exactly where the song will drop.
* **Bulk Loading:** Drag an entire folder from your computer, and it populates your setlist instantly.

### 🚀 Live Performance Tools
* **Smart Auto-Scroll:** 60fps smooth scrolling logic. Adjust speed (1-15) to match the song's tempo perfectly.
* **Reference Tuner:** Built-in audio generator for standard tuning (E A D G B E). Tune by ear without needing external hardware.
* **Chord Library:** A comprehensive database of chords (Naturals, Sharps, Flats) with accurate, clean fingering diagrams (no confusing numbers, just visual dots).

### 🎨 UI & Experience
* **Theme Switcher:** Toggle between **Stage Dark Mode** (default) and Light Mode.
* **Social Hub:** Quick access links to YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, and Apple Music to remember the actual song :)
* **Responsive:** Works on Laptops, Tablets, and iPads.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & How it Works (RAM)
YourGuitarGig runs entirely **Client-Side** in your browser.

* **Zero Data Collection:** No files are ever uploaded to a server.
* **100% Private:** When you load your songs, they are stored temporarily in your device's **RAM** (Random Access Memory).
* **Transient Session:** Because we respect your privacy and speed, **closing the tab wipes the data**. 
    * *Tip:* Keep your songs in a folder on your computer. When you're ready to play, just drag the folder into the app, and you are ready in seconds!

## 📖 How to Use

1.  **Open the App:** Visit the link (or open `index.html` locally).
2.  **Load Songs:** Click **"📄 Files"** to select specific sheets or **"📂 Folder"** to load a whole directory.
3.  **Organize:**
    * Create "Setlists" using the **+** button.
    * Drag and drop songs to change the order.
    * Use the ✎ icon to rename songs inside the app.
4.  **Play:** Select a song and hit **Spacebar** or the **Play Button** to start scrolling.
5.  **Tools:** Click the Hamburger Menu (☰) to access the **Tuner** or **Chords**.

## 💻 System Requirements
* **OS:** Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS (iPad), Android.
* **Browser:** Any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
* **Hardware:** No special hardware required. Works on low-end laptops and tablets.
* **Internet:** Only required to load the page initially. Once loaded, it runs **Offline**.
